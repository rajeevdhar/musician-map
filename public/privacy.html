MUSICIANMAPPER.

Privacy Policy

Last updated October 31, 2026

MusicianMapper (musicianmapper.com) helps musicians find each other on a map. This page explains what we store, who can see it, which outside services are involved, and how to delete your data. We don't sell your data, we don't show ads, and we don't use analytics or advertising trackers.

What we collect

Your account. If you sign up with email, we store your email address and a securely hashed password. If you sign in with Google, Google shares your name, email address and profile picture with us. We use your name and email; we don't display your Google picture.

Your public profile. Everything you put on your profile can be seen by anyone who visits the site, signed in or not:

  • Display name, bio, instruments, genres, skill level, gear, availability, years playing, what you're looking for, and whether you offer lessons
  • Links you add (website, Instagram, YouTube, SoundCloud, Bandcamp, TikTok), your profile photo and any audio clip you upload
  • Your approximate location and area name. When you choose "City / zip area", your position is rounded to roughly 1 km before it's saved, so your exact address is never stored. If you choose "Hidden from map", no location is stored at all.
  • When you were last active, used to show an "online" indicator

Private contact details. The contact email and phone number you add are not public. Only you can see them, plus anyone whose contact request you accept.

Messages and activity. We store the messages you send and receive, contact requests, the musicians you save (and any notes on them), the people you block, and any reports you file. Messages are visible to you and the other person. The site administrator can access stored data when needed to investigate a report or abuse, or when required by law.

On your device. Your browser stores your sign-in session and a few display preferences (theme, text size, whether you've seen the intro tour). These are needed for the site to work and aren't used for tracking.

How we use it

  • To show your profile on the map and let other musicians find and contact you
  • To deliver messages and contact requests
  • To keep the site safe: new photos are checked for nudity on your own device before upload, your name and bio are screened for hateful, sexual or violent content when you save your profile, and we review reports from other users

Services we rely on

  • Supabase stores the database, accounts, photos and audio (servers in the eastern US).
  • Railway hosts the website.
  • Google handles sign-in, only if you choose "Continue with Google". The site's font is also loaded from Google Fonts, so Google sees your IP address when the page loads.
  • OpenAI screens your display name and bio for harmful content when you save your profile. OpenAI says it doesn't use data sent to its API to train its models.
  • OpenStreetMap provides the map images and location search. When you search for a place or use your current location, the search text or coordinates are sent to OpenStreetMap's servers.

Blocking and reporting

If you block someone, neither of you can message or send contact requests to the other, and they can no longer see your contact details. Reports are anonymous: the person you report isn't told who reported them.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your data while your account exists. You can edit your profile or hide yourself from the map at any time. To delete everything, open your profile and choose Delete account. This permanently removes your account, profile, photos, audio, messages, contact requests, saved musicians, blocks and reports you filed. Copies may remain in our service providers' backups for a short time before they're overwritten.

You can also email us to ask for a copy of your data or to have it deleted.

Age requirement

You must be 16 or older to create an account. If we learn that someone under 16 has an account, we'll delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
